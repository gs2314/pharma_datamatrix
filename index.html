    How the scan is captured

    Keystrokes are intercepted at keydown level while the scan box is focused, so the invisible FNC1 bytes (emitted by the scanner as Ctrl+] or as an Alt+numpad 029 sequence) land in our buffer as real separators. The payload is then parsed into its GS1 Application Identifiers — 01 GTIN, 17 expiry, 10 batch, 21 serial — validated against the GS1 General Specifications (§3.2 for lengths, §7.11 for the AI-82 character set, GTIN Mod-10 check digit, YYMMDD with the DD=00 last-of-month convention), and re-emitted as a clean pure-digits-and-letters string for the clipboard in the canonical order 01 · 17 · 10 · 21. That order puts the only two variable-length AIs (batch and serial) on opposite sides of the fixed prefix 21, so the HMNO / EMVS dirty parser on the paste side can split them unambiguously even with FNC1 stripped.

    Each parked row has two copy buttons: Copy sends the canonical pure-digit payload (the form the gov portal wants); Raw sends the untouched scan including \u001D (for receivers that honor FNC1 — rare in web portals, common in ERP systems).
